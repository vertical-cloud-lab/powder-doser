# Chain test plan, v3 (kinematic dock, guide, doors, latches, enclosure)

What v3 has to answer, on top of v2's chain questions: **does the electronics carriage park repeatably in its guide, rise square, seat on every module's kinematic dock every time, hold that dock through the tilt, the dose and the taps, open the outlet door only while it holds the module, and let go cleanly; do the latches hold the plates on the top run and open only at the station; and does the enclosure keep drafts off?** Numbers in *italics* are predictions from the CAD checks ([`checks/`](checks)) and the simulations ([sim/README.md](sim/README.md)); a result far from them means the model or the build is wrong.

Run in order. T0-T3 are v2's chain tests, unchanged. Log with [`test/chain_rig.py`](test/chain_rig.py) (CSV under `runs/`; `--sim` for a dry run) and post a summary in [#128](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/powder-doser/issues/128).

| T0 | **Fit, by hand** | Drive chain off, all 13 carriages and modules on, the EC parked | Turn the loop one lap by hand; feeler gauge at the corners, and watch each latch follower ride the station's rail | No contact or binding. Expect a jam to shift the dock unless each seat ball has about 8 N of preload* |
| T6d | Backlash and runout | As v2's T6d | TIR; backlash | As v2: under 0.1 mm TIR, backlash 0.1-0.25 mm (the dock sets the same 32.25 mm centre distance) |
| T7 | **Outlet door** | Module 1 at the station; a feeler gauge across the exit; a strobe or video | The finger's contact point; the shutter's travel at the dock (*6 mm*); does it shut fully when the EC parks; the force on the push strip (spring gauge) | Opens only in the dock's last 6 mm, shuts completely before the EC parks (*simulated: shut 14 mm before it parks, about 4 mm after the balls leave their seats*); push strip force 0.3-0.8 N (*0.27 N shut, 0.63 N open, 0.65 N on the finger in MuJoCo*). ## What the results decide

- **T6a-b decide the seat magnets.** Their pull is an estimate: if the cone and vee don't hold the EC seated on their own at tilt 0 with the electromagnet off, use the bigger magnets before anything else.
- **T6 and T6c test the simulations' fixes** (sim/README.md): the P16's base stop and spring, and the seat magnets' preload. Fit the stop before the first powered dock.
- **T7 decides whether the door is the right answer to Sam's contamination worry** (item 4): a door that shuts every time, with nothing in its grooves after 100 cycles, makes the lap test (T9) a formality.
- **T8 can't weigh doses yet.** The scale fit (README) is the next design change; it waits for Sam's call.

## Logged columns
