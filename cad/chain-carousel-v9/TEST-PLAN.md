# Chain test plan, v9 (v7 with no draft cage: the balance on a granite block, the beaker on the pan 32 mm under the exit, Sam's 2026-11-17 note)

v7 is the main version; v9 is a variation on it. What v9 has to answer, on top of v7's: **does a 0.1 mg balance with its draft
cage off read true inside the closed enclosure (the bay window and the weighing chamber taking the cage's place), with the
carousel stopped and while it runs; does the beaker on the pan, 32 mm under the exit and 2.8 mm under the parked EC's slim door
finger, clear everything that passes over it; does the slim finger still open the outlet door; does the solenoid behind the
gear still tap the auger; and does the balance on its granite block stay clear of the P16?** v7's questions about the station
(the one P16, the guides, the dock on posts, the latch) stand unchanged. Numbers in *italics* are predictions from the CAD
([`checks/`](checks), `layout.p16_7_ext`); v9's station has not been simulated.

Run in order. T0-T3 are v2's chain tests, unchanged. Log with [`test/chain_rig.py`](test/chain_rig.py) (CSV under `runs/`;
`--sim` for a dry run) and post a summary in [#128](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/powder-doser/issues/128).

## What the results decide

- **T8a and T8c decide whether the cage can go:** the closed enclosure (the bay window and the weighing chamber) has to keep a
  0.1 mg balance as quiet as its own draft cage did. If not, the fallback is the cage's glass walls without its top door (the
  stream comes in from above), which costs nothing in drop height.
- **T5, T7 and T8e decide the 32 mm drop:** the beaker's rim 2.8 mm under the parked finger, the door strips 10 mm over it. If
  either is too tight in practice, lower the block 5 mm (37 mm drop) with a thinner paver; nothing else changes.
- **T8d decides the solenoid's new place** behind the gear.
- v7's T4, T4b, T6, T6a and T6c decide the station, as for v7.
