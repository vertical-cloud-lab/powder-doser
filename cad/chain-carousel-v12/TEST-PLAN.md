# Chain test plan, v12 (v11 with the spring-loaded pinion: a cradle, a 1 N/mm spring, a set-screw stop, a hall switch and the seek; the EC's second seat; Sterling's 2026-11-27 request)

v7 is the main version; v12 is v11 with the spring-loaded pinion. What v12 has to answer, on top of v11's: **does the cradle let a
gear that lands tip-on-tip lift the pinion instead of jamming the index; does the seek (forward, back; a second try; a re-index)
always bring it into mesh, and does the hall switch say so correctly; does the spring hold the pinion in tight mesh at every tilt
while the NEMA 11 stalls before any tooth jumps; and does the second seat keep the parked EC level between modules?** The model
([`sim/README.md`](sim/README.md)) says: 0 jams in 504 simulated indexes, about 1 arrival in 4 on the tips, each cleared by the first
seek with the teeth lubricated. Numbers in *italics* are predictions from the CAD ([`checks/`](checks)) and the MuJoCo model. v11's
rows below are unchanged except T0, T6, T6a, T6d and T7, and the new T6e-T6g.

Run in order. T0-T3 are v2's chain tests, unchanged. Log with [`test/chain_rig.py`](test/chain_rig.py) (CSV under `runs/`;
`--sim` for a dry run) and post a summary in [#128](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/powder-doser/issues/128).
`chain_rig.py` drives v11's P16-150 from v11's table (`P16_TABLE`, 0-90 deg), frees the NEMA 11 during every index, and after every index runs v12's seek (`Rig.seek_mesh`) and logs the cradle's switch before and after (`hall_before`, `seeks`, `hall_after`).

## What the results decide

- **T6a and T6b decide the straight rail and the mesh at rest:** with the hinge 5 mm nearer the carriage, every gear has to meet
  the pinion at rest. If neither mode (free or seeking) is clean, the rail's mouth can flare more, or the pinion's tips can be
  chamfered so the gear slides in more gently.
- **T5, T6c and T6d decide 0-90°:** the P16-150 through the front plate, its 30 mm lever at 90°, and the hinge 73 mm behind the front
  chain.
- **T7 decides the rod door,** T8d the solenoid's new place, and T12 the wiring path.
- **T8, T8a and T8c decide the tall-cage balance and the 33 mm drop.**
- v7's T4 and T4b decide the latch, as for v7; T4c checks that nothing lifts the module.
