# Chain test plan, v7 (v6's ladder and long plate, one P16 under the station, the dock on posts, Sam's 2026-11-10 notes)

What v7 has to answer, on top of v6's: **does the one P16-150, leaning 44.5 deg from its cross bar behind the balance, lift the
EC 10 mm out of its guides onto the posts' domes and then turn the docked EC and module 0-90 deg (130 of its 150 mm at 90 deg);
do the guides' funnels and cone/vee seats park the EC in the same place every time; do the magnets in the printed posts hold
the dock; does the latch's longer spring pocket work at the cam's 4.5 mm and the 8 mm hand release; and does the dose fall
through the dosing lid's 30 mm hole at every tilt without letting drafts into the balance?** v5's questions (the balance,
drafts, the dose) stand. Numbers in *italics* are predictions from the CAD ([`checks/`](checks), `layout.p16_7_ext`); v7's
station has not been simulated.

Run in order. T0-T3 are v2's chain tests, unchanged. Log with [`test/chain_rig.py`](test/chain_rig.py) (CSV under `runs/`;
`--sim` for a dry run) and post a summary in [#128](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/powder-doser/issues/128).

## What the results decide

- **T6 and T6c decide v7's station:** one P16 doing both the lift and the tilt again (v2-v4's idea), now leaning 44.5 deg from
  behind the balance. During the lift the guides take about 0.7 of its push sideways; if the shoes bind, the next step is a
  steeper P16 (a taller balance clearance) or a separate lift.
- **T6a decides the printed dock on posts:** printed domes and a printed flat at the tips of three 8 x 8 mm posts, held only
  by magnets dropped in at a print pause.
- **T4 and T4b decide the latch:** the shelf (2.5 mm under the keeper at the cam) and whether a stock spring fits the pocket.
- **T8, T8a and T8c decide the balance and the dosing lid:** whether it reads true under the running carousel with a hole in
  its draft cage's top.
