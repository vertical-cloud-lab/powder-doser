# Chain test plan, v10 (v9 with the station inside the loop: one P16-100 pulls the module up, a keyed rail instead of the dock, the beaker 18 mm under the exit; Sam's 2026-11-19 note)

v7 is the main version; v10 is a variation on v9, and so on v7. What v10 has to answer, on top of v9's: **does a printed
T-bracket sliding into the EC's T-slot bring every module's 44T gear into mesh with the pinion, index after index, with no dock
and no magnets, and hold it there under the auger's torque; does one P16-100 inside the loop, fully extended between doses, pull
the EC and the module up and let them back down; does the EC's shoe in its funnel seat keep the EC in place between modules; does
the ramp on the EC open each outlet door and the spring shut it; does the latch let go before the rail lifts the module; does
every dose fall 18 mm through the deck's 16 mm hole into the beaker; and do the wires from inside the loop survive the tilting on
their way out through the front side plate?** v9's questions about the balance with no draft cage (T8a, T8c) stand. The tilt
range is 4° (coupled, between doses) to the top tilt, *65°* (*the clash check's limit: clear at 4-65°, at 70° the EC's front meets the front chain's A-1 link; see README*). Numbers in *italics*
are predictions from the CAD ([`checks/`](checks), `layout.p16_10_ext`, `parts10.module_lift`, `parts10.door_open_at`); v10's
station has not been simulated.

Run in order. T0-T3 are v2's chain tests, unchanged. Log with [`test/chain_rig.py`](test/chain_rig.py) (CSV under `runs/`;
`--sim` for a dry run) and post a summary in [#128](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/powder-doser/issues/128).
`chain_rig.py` drives v10's P16-100 from v10's table (`P16_TABLE`, 4-65 deg) and frees the NEMA 11 during every index (`--seek` turns it slowly instead).

## What the results decide

- **T6a and T6b decide the keyed rail:** a T-bracket on every plate and the EC's ramps have to bring the gear into mesh at
  every index, with no dock. If neither mode (free or seeking) is clean, the ramps can be made longer so the gear comes into
  mesh more slowly; the latch cam's flat then has to grow with them (T4c).
- **T6, T6c and T6d decide the station inside the loop:** one P16-100 pulling the EC and the module up, the shoe in its funnel
  seat, and the rail taking the mesh's force that the dock and its magnets took in v7-v9.
- **T7 decides the pulled door,** and T12 the wiring path out of the loop.
- **T8, T8b and T8e decide the 18 mm drop:** the deck 1.5 mm under the passing tubes with a 16 mm hole, the beaker's rim 1 mm
  under the deck. If either is too tight in use, a thinner block lowers the beaker (5 mm thinner: a 23 mm drop); nothing else
  changes.
- **T8a and T8c decide whether the cage can go,** as for v9.
- v7's T4 and T4b decide the latch, as for v7; T4c decides its longer cam.
