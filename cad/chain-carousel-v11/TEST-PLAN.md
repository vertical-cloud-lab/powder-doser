# Chain test plan, v11 (v10 with 0-90°: longer carriages, a P16-150 through the front side plate, a straight rail, the door opened by the EC, the tall-cage balance; Sam's 2026-11-25 notes)

v7 is the main version; v11 is a variation on v10, and so on v7. What v11 has to answer, on top of v10's: **does one P16-150, its
base outside the front side plate on the front bar, pull the EC and the module from 0° to 90° and back through the plate's slot;
with the hinge 5 mm nearer the carriage, does every 44T gear meet the 24T pinion at rest, index after index, with the T sliding
straight through the rail and nothing lifting it; does the EC's front ramp open every door through its rod, and the spring shut it;
do the solenoid's taps over clip 1 move the powder better than v10's over clip 2; and does the dose fall 33 mm through the dosing
lid into the beaker on its pillar inside the balance's tall cage?** The tilt range is 0° (at rest, between doses) to *90°* (*the clash
check is clear at every angle from 0 to 90°; see README*). Numbers in *italics* are predictions from the CAD ([`checks/`](checks),
`layout.p16_11_ext`, `layout.p16_11_lever`, `parts11.door_open_at`); v11's station has not been simulated.

Run in order. T0-T3 are v2's chain tests, unchanged. Log with [`test/chain_rig.py`](test/chain_rig.py) (CSV under `runs/`;
`--sim` for a dry run) and post a summary in [#128](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/powder-doser/issues/128).
`chain_rig.py` drives v11's P16-150 from v11's table (`P16_TABLE`, 0-90 deg) and frees the NEMA 11 during every index (`--seek` turns it slowly instead).

## What the results decide

- **T6a and T6b decide the straight rail and the mesh at rest:** with the hinge 5 mm nearer the carriage, every gear has to meet
  the pinion at rest. If neither mode (free or seeking) is clean, the rail's mouth can flare more, or the pinion's tips can be
  chamfered so the gear slides in more gently.
- **T5, T6c and T6d decide 0-90°:** the P16-150 through the front plate, its 30 mm lever at 90°, and the hinge 73 mm behind the front
  chain.
- **T7 decides the rod door,** T8d the solenoid's new place, and T12 the wiring path.
- **T8, T8a and T8c decide the tall-cage balance and the 33 mm drop.**
- v7's T4 and T4b decide the latch, as for v7; T4c checks that nothing lifts the module.
