balloons in `renders/assembly_exploded_bom.png`, `renders/assembly_bom_callouts.png`

`assembly/bom.csv`.  Electronics, wiring and the balance are not in the CAD;
see the SI bill of materials in PR #97.  Item 1 is whatever the doser is

4. Servo screws and nuts (items 5, 6)
5. Servo pinions (items 7, 8)
6. Mounting plate onto the hinge (item 9)
7. Hinge screws and locknuts (items 10, 11)
8. Stepper (items 12, 13)
9. Stepper pinion (item 14)
10. Tap-collar base (items 6, 15, 16, 17, 18)
11. Auger with brackets and tap collar (items 19, 20, 21)
12. Bracket and collar screws (items 5, 6, 22)
13. Solenoid (items 23, 24)
14. Auger cap (item 25)
