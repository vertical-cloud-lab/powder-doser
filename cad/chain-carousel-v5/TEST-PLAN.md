# Chain test plan, v5 (v4 with the HR-202i under the station)

What v5 has to answer, on top of v4's: **does the dose land in the beaker on the balance, with the balance reading true while the carousel runs over it; do the two P16s lift the bar together within their 2 mm of lost motion; and do the guided shoes bring the EC up square without v4's cradle?** v4's questions, kept below: **does EC v4 reach round the auger and seat on the plate's auger-side dock every time; does that dock hold through the tilt, the dose and a jam with no electromagnet, only the module's weight and three magnets; does the pin latch hold every plate while it hangs under the bottom run and let go only at the station, with the module settling on its tube rest; and can an auger be pulled off its plate at the side hatch with the side tabs, not the chain, taking the pull?** Numbers in *italics* are predictions from the CAD checks ([`checks/`](checks)) and the simulations ([sim/README.md](sim/README.md)); a result far from them means the model or the build is wrong.

Run in order. T0-T3 are v2's chain tests, unchanged. Log with [`test/chain_rig.py`](test/chain_rig.py) (CSV under `runs/`; `--sim` for a dry run) and post a summary in [#128](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/powder-doser/issues/128).

## What the results decide

- **T6c decides v4's dock.** v3 holds its mesh with a switchable electromagnet; v4 has no room for one and relies on the module's weight and three magnets. If a jam opens the dock before the motor stalls, the preload has to grow before anything else is tested.
- **T4 decides the pin latch:** the plate hangs from it under the bottom run, so a latch that backs off drops a module.
- **T11 decides whether item 7 pays off:** an auger off and on in under a minute, with the carriage held by the frame, is the reason v4 exists.
- **T8 and T8a decide v5:** whether a balance under the carousel reads true while the carousel runs. If it can't, the balance needs its own stand, or the station has to stop everything while it reads.
- **T6 decides the lift bar:** two P16s that drift apart by more than their 2 mm of lost motion would twist the bar and load the dock.
