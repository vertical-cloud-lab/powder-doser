# Chain test plan, v4 (the augers outside the loop)

What v4 has to answer, on top of [v3's](../chain-carousel-v3/TEST-PLAN.md) questions: **does EC v4 reach round the auger and seat on the plate's auger-side dock every time; does that dock hold through the tilt, the dose and a jam with no electromagnet, only the module's weight and three magnets; does the pin latch hold every plate while it hangs under the bottom run and let go only at the station, with the module settling on its tube rest; and can an auger be pulled off its plate at the side hatch with the side tabs, not the chain, taking the pull?** Numbers in *italics* are predictions from the CAD checks ([`checks/`](checks)) and the simulations ([sim/README.md](sim/README.md)); a result far from them means the model or the build is wrong.

Run in order. T0-T3 are v2's chain tests, unchanged. Log with [`test/chain_rig.py`](test/chain_rig.py) (CSV under `runs/`; `--sim` for a dry run) and post a summary in [#128](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/powder-doser/issues/128).

Every latch opens on the rail, its module drops onto the rest and is picked up again as the carriage leaves. *`check_interference.py --sweep`: as drawn, the augers passing the station hit the parked EC's arms 2 links either side of it; park the EC 28-30 mm under its dock first (README, Checks)* |

| T4 | **Pin latch and tube rest** | Carriage 1 at the station, carriage 2 half an index away, both on the bottom run; force gauge on module 2's free (cap) end | The pull that moves a latched module away from its carriage (module 2); the pin's travel on the rail; the module's drop onto the tube rest with its latch open (module 1) | A latched module holds at least 10 N either way without the pin moving (*v2's loop model: up to 5.6 N at 100 mm/s, 4.4 N static*); the open pin is clear of the keeper's hole; module 1 drops 0.5 mm onto the rest (*CAD: 0.5 mm*) and every pin springs home off the rail's ramps |

There is no electromagnet. *Simulated (sim/README.md): a ball lifts at 2.3-3.2 N of separating force, under the 3.7 N stall at 450 mA, and the EC slides on its seats once tilted. Expect this test to fail as drawn.* Add preload before anything else (about 8 N per ball, or a coil), and try a lower current limit while dosing |
| T6d | Backlash and runout | As v2's T6d | TIR; backlash | As v2: under 0.1 mm TIR, backlash 0.1-0.25 mm (the dock sets the same 32.25 mm centre distance, on the auger's far side) |

## What the results decide

- **T6c decides v4's dock.** v3 holds its mesh with a switchable electromagnet; v4 has no room for one and relies on the module's weight and three magnets. If a jam opens the dock before the motor stalls, the preload has to grow before anything else is tested.
- **T4 decides the pin latch:** the plate hangs from it under the bottom run, so a latch that backs off drops a module.
- **T11 decides whether item 7 pays off:** an auger off and on in under a minute, with the carriage held by the frame, is the reason v4 exists.
- **T8 can't weigh doses yet** (README, item 6).
