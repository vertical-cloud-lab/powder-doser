# CalculiX FEA of the carousel parts

Work in progress (PR #185 follow-up). Every structural part of chain carousel v2
(`../chain-carousel-v2/`) and the paternoster (`../paternoster/`) meshed with gmsh
(second-order tetrahedra) from the same CadQuery source the CAD uses, solved with
CalculiX 2.21 (`ccx`), for deflection, stress, safety factor and natural frequency.
Results, plots and the method go here when the runs are done.
