# Chain test plan, v8 (v7 with EC v8 behind the balance, the exit 50 mm over the beaker; Sam's 2026-11-12 note)

What v8 has to answer, on top of v7's: **does EC v8 (Sam's Aug 27 layout: the solenoid just in front of the gear, the NEMA 11
behind it) dock on the posts behind the gear and drive the auger; does the outlet door open through its 60 mm push bar; does
the P16-150, now pinned under the solenoid's cradle and leaning 32 deg, lift the EC 10 mm and turn it 0-90 deg (126 of its
150 mm); does the balance read true with the beaker on a cup 38 mm off the pan's centre; and does a dose fall the 50 mm from
the exit into the beaker without straying?** Numbers in *italics* are predictions from the CAD ([`checks/`](checks),
`layout.p16_8_ext`); v8's station has not been simulated.

Run in order. T0-T3 are v2's chain tests, unchanged. Log with [`test/chain_rig.py`](test/chain_rig.py) (CSV under `runs/`;
`--sim` for a dry run) and post a summary in [#128](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/powder-doser/issues/128).

## What the results decide

- **T6, T6c and T8d decide v8's station** (v7's notes follow): one P16 doing both the lift and the tilt again (v2-v4's idea), now leaning 44.5 deg from
  behind the balance. During the lift the guides take about 0.7 of its push sideways; if the shoes bind, the next step is a
  steeper P16 (a taller balance clearance) or a separate lift.
- **T6a decides the printed dock on posts:** printed domes and a printed flat at the tips of three 8 x 8 mm posts, held only
  by magnets dropped in at a print pause.
- **T4 and T4b decide the latch:** the shelf (2.5 mm under the keeper at the cam) and whether a stock spring fits the pocket.
- **T8, T8a and T8c decide the balance and the dosing lid:** whether it reads true under the running carousel with a hole in
  its draft cage's top.
