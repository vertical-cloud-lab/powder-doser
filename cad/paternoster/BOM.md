- Cut the two side plates and the motor plate from `dxf/` (1/4 in 6061).
- Re-bore four 35BS32 sprockets to 35 mm H7 for two 6202-2RS each (top idlers).

- Cut the keyed bar to 435 mm and 463 mm.
