`qc.valid_for_cross_powder_comparison = false`, `verdict = demo`. **Not**

## Result

| target | delivered | error | status | time | cycles | taps | auger rev |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5000 g | **0.4963 g** | **−3.7 mg** | **ok** | 100.0 s | bulk 0, fine 16, tap 2 | 4 | 2.00 |

Inside the ±5 mg tolerance on the first try. The frozen salt-tuned three-phase
controller handled it cleanly.

**The dose started in the fine phase, not bulk** — this is the controller's own
threshold rule, not a fault. For a 0.5 g target, `dose_thresholds_for` sets
`t1 = min(0.5, 0.5) = 0.5 g`, and the controller leaves bulk once
`remaining ≤ t1 + anticipation (0.12 g) = 0.62 g`. The dose starts at 0.5 g to
go, already below 0.62 g, so bulk is skipped and all delivery is by 45° fine
increments (~30 mg each at salt's ~217 mg/rev) followed by a 2-tap trim. Fine
carried 0.4709 g over 16 cycles; the tap phase closed the last 29 mg to −3.7 mg
in 2 cycles.

## Environment

Pre-run survey (180 s): 0 mechanical shock events, jitter 0.165 mg (drafts
below display resolution), +2.9 mg/min smooth drift. That is a strict Block G
stand-down by the launch gate (180 s error ~11 mg > ±5 mg), but the disturbance
was pure smooth drift with zero shocks, which the per-phase tares absorb over a
short dose. Confirmed by the run itself: `META,env.*` reported **0 shock events,
0 retries, 0 disturbed trials, 0.0000 g drift corrected**. The artifact-rejection
machinery had nothing to do.

This is why the demo is reported as a demo and not promoted into the dataset:
the environment was clean *for this run*, but the room was not in a gate-verified
window, so the −3.7 mg error carries the room's ~±10 mg/180 s uncertainty and
should not be read as a validated ±5 mg accuracy figure. It is a demonstration
that the controller dispenses 0.5 g of salt to target, not a measurement of how
accurately it can.
