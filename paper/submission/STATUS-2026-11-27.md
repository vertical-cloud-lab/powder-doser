  designed by engineers and modelled by AI agents") and abstract v6 (246 words): v5 from the 27 Oct

  three targets, and **new Fig. 8: 103 repeated doses on the stepped-tilt procedure** (PR #131, 18–20 Nov).
- **Storyline (later on 27 Nov):** follows the project page's narrative: the doser, how it was designed,
  how a dose runs, using the doser, 13 powders open loop then closed loop, repeated doses, the alloy
  workflow, limits, and building one (Section 2.10), with many powders as the next step.
- **Tables (main text, 5, numbered in order of first citation, as on the project page):** 1 the 13 powders,
  2 test protocols (PR #150's table), 3 closed loop by target, **4 stepped-tilt repeatability (new)**,
  5 AI tools and models (Experimental). The AI failure-modes table is now in the SI (Section S4).
- **New results text:** Section 2.7 (repeated doses: AlSi10Mg 20 of 20 doses of 2 g within ±5%,
  11 within ±1 mg; 87 of 103 doses within ±5% across five powders) and Section 2.8 (the ten closed-loop
  Al 4047 doses that filled an 8 g charge and two atomizer cups to within 2.5 mg, and how a clog was
  cleared by reversing the auger).
- **SI (26 pp.):** new sections on the stepped-tilt trials and the Al 4047 doses, the v2 exploded view
  with every part, the generative-CAD comparison, the open-loop table for every powder, time per dose,
  and a "hello world" first test with expected numbers.
- **Tilt:** corrected everywhere. The test protocols used 0–45°; the servos tilt further, and the stepped-tilt
  procedure started at 60°, and one clog-clearing attempt went to 75°.
- **Jargon:** none of the terms the audit (PR #149) flagged as misleading is left (ablations, silent
  regressions, interferences, primitives, parity, unit operation, glidant, trickler, feed factor,
  operating envelope); "afterflow" is replaced by plain wording.

- **References:** all 44 cited entries checked against Crossref/DataCite on 27 Nov
  ([`../references_validation.md`](../references_validation.md)): 26 fixed, two preprints switched to
