  nominal bang stop line is target - guard = 1.700 g; 'halt' is true mass there
  bulk ff model                      halt vs stop     med    p95  over%  abort%  tmo%  med t
  prior 0.50 (default), no update         -844 mg    -4.3    9.1     16       7     1     75
  prior 0.50 + bulk EWMA                   -55 mg  -158.4   14.4     17       9    56    301
  prior 0.10 (5x low), no update           121 mg   -59.3  210.0     38      36    57    301

  (m_hat ~ b_hat + r_hat*tau_bal: the input-model ff biases r_hat, and the 0.7 s
   lag deconvolution turns that into the halt position -- high prior = early/safe,
   low prior = late/overshoot.  The EWMA partially rescues a low prior (aborts
   36%->11%), but any bang that halts near the nominal stop line hands the
   trickle a small remaining gap, and there trickle_tap's FIRST-TICK k*sigma
   (~229 mg: the freshly seeded KF's initial covariance, never yet shrunk by a
   measurement) fires the cutoff immediately -- tap_finish is left to close
   0.15-0.28 g at ~1 mg/tap and the dose times out short.)
