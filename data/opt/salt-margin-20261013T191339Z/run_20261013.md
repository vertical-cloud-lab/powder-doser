## The `MemoryError` after long doses

Three doses that ran 417–441 s (the tap budget or the nudge budget ran out) finished
their dose, then failed with `MemoryError` (1.5 KB) while the Pico built the RESULT
line. The firmware reported them as `fw-error` with the delivered mass, and the auger
was already stopped. It happened at the runner's 1st, 17th and 18th dose since boot, so
it is not only heap fragmentation. Doses of 430–441 s with 150–165 telemetry rows
earlier in the session did not fail. With `log_max_rows` at 120 the later long doses
(`bo-003`, 447 s) ended normally as `cycle-budget`.

## Results

**Stop:** the 21:30 deadline (`campaign.json` `stop_reason`). 27 dose records: 2 `not-tared`
(nothing dispensed), 16 corners at 0 mg, 3 `fw-error` (2 checks, 1 BO), 6 usable BO doses.
The cup took about 11.6 g of salt.

**The 16 corners, 35 mg (2026-09-29) → 0 mg (today)**, from `compare_margin.py`:

- The taps took over a median **32 mg** short at 0 mg (n = 14) against **54 mg** at 35 mg
  (n = 49 September doses). The 35 mg cut in the aim moved the handover only about 22 mg,
  because k·σ and the afterflow term still sit between the aim and the handover.
- Where the PI could finish, it did: `corner-03` went from 227 s to **28 s** (no taps at
  all; the trickle landed −3.1 mg) and `corner-10` from 352 s to 72 s.
- Over the 7 corners that ended `ok` both times, time changed by a median −12 s (mean
  −56 s).
- 5 of 16 overshot at 0 mg, against 6 at 35 mg. Three of them (`corner-04`, `-08`,
  `-11`) overshot in the bulk, which the margin does not touch, and `corner-00` and
  `corner-13` stopped overshooting. The two new trickle slugs, `corner-06` (+87 mg) and
  `corner-07` (+103 mg), both combine 30° trim tilt with trim taps.
- `corner-12` and `corner-13` ran out of taps at 0 mg.

**Rig drift:** the tap stage moved much less powder per tap today. The same 15° tap tilt
moved 1.4–2.6 mg per tap at `bo-005` on 10-06 and about 0.2 mg per tap today, with "lip
empty" nudges. So the 0 mg corners carry a day effect as well as the margin, and it
works against them wherever taps do most of the work.
