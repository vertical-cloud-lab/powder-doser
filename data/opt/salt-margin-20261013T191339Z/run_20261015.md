## The `fw-error` fix (firmware `trickle_tap/2026-10-15`)

**What failed.** `"RESULT " + json.dumps(doc)` needs two contiguous blocks of about 1.5 kB
each. After long doses the Pico W's heap could not provide one. The allocation that failed
was always 1549 bytes, and MicroPython had already run its own garbage collection before
raising. The dose itself had finished and the auger had stopped. But the exception left
`_dose` through the dose wrapper, which then reported the dose as `fw-error`, with the last
mass seen.

**How often.** Three doses on 10-13, three today. Lowering `log_max_rows` to 120 on 10-13
made it rarer, but it still happened.

**The fix.**
- `_emit_result` builds the document (`_result_doc`), then hands it to `_log_result`.
- On `MemoryError`, `_log_result` runs a collection and streams the JSON with
  `json.dump(doc, sys.stdout)`, which MicroPython writes piece by piece with no large block.
- The `res` reprint and the refill runner use the same path.
- Reporting only: the dose's behavior does not change.

**Tests.** A new simulation test makes `json.dumps` fail on the RESULT document. It checks
that exactly one RESULT line still goes out with the dose's own status, both streamed to
stdout and through an injected log. All checks pass: 105 trickle-tap, 33 refill-tap,
78 executor and 65 campaign.
