| Study | Feeder-derived measurement | Actual size-related role / conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Bostijn et al. (2019) | Feed-factor maximum and decay (`FFmax`, `FFdecay`), plus gravimetric feed-rate variability and relative error (`RSD`, `RE`) | Laser-diffraction `dv50` was an independently measured input used to predict feeder responses—not an output inferred from gravimetry. (pqac-00000044, pqac-00000060) |
| Fathollahi et al. (2020) | Catch-balance discharged mass at 1 s intervals and a displacement-feed-factor profile characterizing effective-density changes | Particle size was measured separately by laser diffraction. Trace differences were associated jointly with size, compressibility, packing, and density; PSD was not predicted from the balance trace. (pqac-00000014, pqac-00000058) |
| Fathollahi et al. (2024) | LIW data at 1 Hz; feed factor in g/rev, with local and overall trends used to indicate densification and screw layering | PSD was measured separately by dry laser diffraction. Feed-factor trends diagnose feeder phenomena, not particle size or PSD. (pqac-00000045, pqac-00000046, pqac-00000048) |
| Kobayashi et al. (2024) | Feed-factor profile—mass delivered per screw rotation as a function of hopper powder weight | Models predict the feed-factor profile from hopper weight, API fraction, screw speed, and optionally independently measured properties including `D10`, `D50`, and `D90`; they do not invert feed factor to obtain PSD. (pqac-00000047, pqac-00000049, pqac-00000050) |
| Jaspers et al. (2025) | Feed factor under different feeder configurations | The finest lactose grade had the lowest feed factor, but grades with nearly identical PSDs behaved differently, showing that size is not uniquely recoverable from feed factor. (pqac-00000061) |
| Archary et al. (2016) | Feeder speed/feed factor supported coal mass-flow estimation | Online PSD/fineness came from a separate laser particle-size probe and was validated against sampling and sieving—not inferred from feeder gravimetry. (pqac-00000005, pqac-00000008, pqac-00000009) |
| **Overall finding** | **Balance mass-time, mass-flow fluctuations, or mass per revolution** | **None of these studies predicts particle size or PSD from feeder gravimetry; size is independently measured, used as an input/covariate, or obtained from a separate optical sensor.** |
